Checkboxes switch one thing on or off; radios pick one of a few options.

**Checkbox.** A `frame-bg` square, `radius-md`, with a `check-mark` tick when on. The label sits 5px to its right. When a checkbox enables a score, its label is `accent-green` when on and `disabled` when off.

**Radio.** A `frame-bg` circle with a `check-mark` dot when selected. Used for Fair / D / R direction.

**Rules.** Use radios for two to four options that are always visible. Label party options "D" and "R" in `body` text; do not colour them `dem` and `gop`, because colour here would read as state.

The consumer provides the label and the current value.
