The score row switches one score on and sets its weight; every score is always listed, and each row opens and closes on its own.

**Closed (one line).** A checkbox, the score name (`accent-green` when on, `body` when off), one short phrase saying what the score favors in `muted`, and the weight or "off" at the right. The phrase is at most about 45 characters and never wraps.

**Open.** Below the line: a weight slider about 140 to 240px wide with a number field, an Aim choice (Fair, D, R) for directional scores, and an "Open chart" button. Opening one row does not close the others.

**Grouping.** Rows sit under three `subheading` labels: Geography and population, Elections, Demographics. The panel heading shows how many are on.

**Unavailable scores.** A score whose data is missing stays in the list, unchecked, and its phrase says what it needs ("Needs a reference plan").

**Replaces.** The old row put a 500px slider, the word "Weight" and two unlabelled icon buttons on every score.

The consumer provides the name, the phrase, whether it is on, the weight and any direction.
